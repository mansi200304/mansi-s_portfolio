## Origin · Ownership

My journey into cybersecurity began when a roommate introduced me to ethical hacking. I switched lanes from finance to security, fascinated by how systems work — and how they fail.

Then it became personal. My social media account was hacked, and I never recovered my data. Later, over $1,000 disappeared after my card was skimmed at a restaurant. I investigated the suspicious transactions, traced a common network indicator, secured my accounts, and recovered every dollar.

Those experiences shaped my approach: follow the evidence, question assumptions, and keep investigating until the pieces make sense.

They also shaped how I approach software engineering. I want to understand how a system behaves, build something useful, test its assumptions, and make it more reliable and secure.
